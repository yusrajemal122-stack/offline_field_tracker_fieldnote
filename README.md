1. Project Overview

Fieldnote is a React-based web application developed by Yusra Jemal to make recording and viewing field-related issues more organized and accessible.

The project explores a practical challenge: how can people keep track of important field issues when internet connectivity is unreliable?

Fieldnote is built around the idea that recording and managing information should be simple, organized, and accessible, even in environments where connectivity may be limited.

2. The Problem

Field activities often take place in environments where reliable internet access cannot be guaranteed.

Depending on traditional paper records or online-only systems can make information management difficult. Records may become disorganized, information may be difficult to retrieve, and field activities may be interrupted by connectivity problems.

Fieldnote explores a digital alternative to these challenges by providing a dedicated application for recording and viewing reported issues.

3. Project Objectives

- Provide a digital space for recording field issues.
- Make recorded information easier to access and review.
- Reduce dependence on disorganized paper-based records.
- Explore the possibilities of offline-capable issue tracking.
- Develop a practical and accessible solution to field information management.

4. Current Features

The current implementation includes:

- Issue Recording: Users can record field-related issues through the application.
- Issue Viewing: Users can view recorded issues through the application.

Additional functionality will be documented as it is implemented and verified.

5. Technology Stack

- Frontend Framework: React

6. Offline Functionality

Offline operation is a central goal of Fieldnote.

The application's actual offline data persistence and behavior are still being verified. This includes checking whether records remain available without an internet connection and whether they are preserved after refreshing the application.

No automatic synchronization capability is claimed in this version.
